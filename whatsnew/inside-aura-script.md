# Inside Aura: the What's New film (draft 3, 20 Nov 2026: conversational, everything explained in full)

**Length:** about 3:15 to 3:30, 9:16 vertical. Every feature is explained in full sentences (owner, 20 Nov), which runs well past the 2:00 target. Length is settled before any footage is made.
**Who it's for:** current users who know Aura as a reading, Transcribe and translate app, plus new visitors.
**Ending:** "Go to aurabyrevyve.com to get started." There's no price, pass or credits wording anywhere.

## The format

This is a real person in a real-looking world, with Aura appearing as a 3D character, the way Detective Pikachu puts a 3D character into real footage.

- **Tolu, the user:** a photoreal Nigerian university student, filmed like Two Heads, with real skin, real light and real acting.
- **Aura, the mascot:** made from the brand art as a soft, slightly plush 3D purple character with the same shape, face and colour as the logo mascot. Aura is the only thing in the film that isn't real. You approve its look before any footage is made.
- **"Inside the app":** a dark lilac space, like a film studio's LED stage. Huge glowing panels surround Tolu, and each panel plays a **real screen recording of the live Aura app**. Light from the panels falls on her face. When Aura jumps on a button inside a panel, we cut to a full-screen close-up of the real recording of that tap.
- **App screens are never generated or redrawn.** They are composited from our recordings after filming, the same rule as Two Heads.
- **Voices:**
  - Aura speaks in the app's own voice, Heart. I make it locally for free, so its pacing is exact and Aura never needs lip-sync.
  - Tolu has one fixed native Nigerian voice from the studio, with lip-sync.
- **Why this format, and not the others:**
  - Two Heads style (people in a room around a laptop) can't show "walking inside the app".
  - A cartoon is out, because you asked for a real video.
  - This format keeps the app 100% real and still delivers the magic of being inside it.

## Characters

- **Tolu:** 20, a 200-level student at a Lagos university. Natural hair in neat braids, a lilac hoodie (Aura's colour) and light jeans. Warm and expressive, but small true reactions, no mugging. She has used Aura for months to listen to her notes. This is a dramatisation and is never presented as a real user.
- **Aura:** small enough to sit on a pillow at home, about knee-high inside the app. It bounces when it moves and its eyes do the acting. Excited, warm and quick.

## Voice spellings (what the voices say)

Written normally here and in the captions. In the voice text: Aura = `Or-rah`, WAEC = `Wah-yek`, IELTS = `I. E. L. T. S.`, JAMB = one word, NECO = one word.

## Script

The film is written as a real conversation: Tolu asks what she would honestly want to know, and Aura answers in full sentences, explaining what each thing is and why it helps. Each scene shows the picture first, then the lines. **[REC: …]** names the real recording that plays.

### 1. The bedroom

Night, in Tolu's neat hostel room. She lies on her bed with her phone up and earbuds in. On the phone, Aura is reading her notes, with each word lighting up. **[REC: read aloud with words lighting up]** Aura pops out of the screen in a puff of purple light and lands on her pillow. Tolu jumps.

> **AURA:** Hey Tolu! You already know I can read your books and notes out loud to you, write out your recordings with Transcribe, and translate your documents, yeah?

> **TOLU** (laughing, hand on her chest): Of course! You read my notes to me every night.

> **AURA:** Well, I want to show you something super cool. Aura can do so much more now. Come, let me show you!

Aura turns to the camera. This is the only look into the lens.

> **AURA:** Are you preparing for JAMB, WAEC, IELTS, or are you a student in the university? Aura is here to make you excel furiously.

Aura grabs Tolu's finger. The phone screen glows huge, and both of them are pulled in with a whoosh.

### 2. Inside Aura

Tolu lands on a glowing lilac floor. Giant panels of the real app rise around her. She turns slowly, mouth open, with the panel light on her face.

> **TOLU:** Wait… where are we? Are we inside Aura?

> **AURA:** Welcome to the new Aura! The first thing I ask is what you're working towards: JAMB or WAEC, university, IELTS, or your work. Then I set up the whole app around your goal. **[REC: choose your goal]**

### 3. Exams

> **TOLU:** Okay, say I'm writing JAMB. Where do I start?

Aura bounces onto the giant "Exams" button, which lights up under its feet.

> **AURA:** If you click here, on Exams, you'll find more than twenty-eight thousand real past questions from JAMB, WAEC and NECO. You can practise one subject, one year, or just the topic you're struggling with. **[REC: exams home, subjects, by year]**

> **TOLU:** Twenty-eight thousand?! And what if I get a question wrong?

A giant question panel. An answer turns red.

> **AURA:** That's my favourite part. I don't just show you the right answer. I teach you that whole topic, right there. I can read the lesson to you while each word lights up, or you can watch it as a video that explains it step by step. **[REC: missed question → quick lesson → Watch it as a video]**

> **TOLU** (leaning in to the video panel): A video lesson, just for the question I missed? Wow!

Aura stands on a giant countdown timer.

> **AURA:** And when you feel ready, you can write a full timed CBT. It looks just like the real JAMB, with the timer and the question numbers, so on exam day nothing surprises you. **[REC: timed CBT]**

> **TOLU:** What about theory questions? Who marks those?

> **AURA:** I do! Type your answer, or take a picture of your handwritten answer. I'll mark it and show you exactly how to score more marks. **[REC: theory marked 18/20]**

### 4. Study plan, oral English, friends and parents

> **TOLU:** This is a lot!

Aura hops across four panels, one for each sentence, like stepping stones.

> **AURA:** There's more! Tell me the date of your exam, and I'll make you a study plan all the way to exam day. If you're writing WAEC English, you can practise your oral English with me, and I'll tell you what to work on. You can start a group mock exam with your friends and see who tops the leaderboard. And your parents can follow your progress with a weekly report. **[REC: study plan · oral English · group mock + leaderboard · parent report]**

> **TOLU** (both hands on her head, laughing): Oh my God, Aura has changed!

### 5. University

> **TOLU:** But I'm already in university. What about me?

Aura slides down into a lecture-hall area of panels.

> **AURA:** When a question is giving you trouble, take a picture of it in Classroom, and I'll show you how to solve it, step by step. If you don't understand a step, just ask me why. **[REC: Classroom workings]**

Aura jumps on the "Transcribe" button.

> **AURA:** And in your lectures, tap Transcribe. I'll write down everything your lecturer says, so you can listen properly instead of rushing to copy, and I'll pull out the key points for you. **[REC: Transcribe a lecture]**

> **TOLU:** So I don't have to write like a machine in class anymore?

> **AURA:** Never again! You can also upload your handout and ask it any question. I'll answer from your own material and show you the exact page I used. I can set you practice questions from your notes, and I can even turn your notes into a video lesson you can watch. **[REC: ask your book with page · practice questions · notes into a video lesson]**

### 6. IELTS

> **TOLU:** My brother is writing IELTS. Can you help him too?

> **AURA:** Of course! He can take a placement test to see where he stands, and practise with full IELTS papers. When he writes his essays, I'll mark them, give him a band score, and tell him exactly what to fix. **[REC: IELTS home, placement, essay marking]**

### 7. Back home

A whoosh, and they pop back out onto Tolu's bed. Her phone glows in her hand.

> **TOLU:** Aura, why didn't you tell me all this before?!

> **AURA** (bouncing on the pillow): I'm telling you now! Go to aurabyrevyve.com and see everything for yourself.

The end card shows the Aura logo and mascot, "See everything Aura can do now", "aurabyrevyve.com", and "Dramatisation" in small text. There is no cast list.

## Notes for the studio

- **Lines:** every line is said word for word. Don't add dialogue.
- **Conversation, not a list:** Aura speaks warmly and naturally, like a friend explaining, with small pauses between sentences. Tolu reacts with her face before she speaks.
- **App screens:** every app picture comes from our real recordings, composited onto the panels with screen glow and used as full-frame inserts. Never generate, redraw or invent any part of the app.
- **Credit costs on screen:** cover any small credit-cost lines that appear in the recordings.
- **Tolu:** her face, hair and outfit stay identical in every shot. Hands are correct, and there are no extra people.
- **Aura's look:** made from the brand mascot art. Lock its look sheet first and get the owner's approval.
- **Timing:** the picture is cut to Aura's voice file, which comes first. Never speed up any voice.
- **Length:** about 26 to 30 shots.
