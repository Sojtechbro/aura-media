# ADA MUST NOT PASS: series bible

A 10-part vertical drama for Aura. 9:16, 30 seconds per episode, Lagos.
Written 19 Nov 2026 by Senami for owner approval. Writing only: nothing generated, spent or posted.
Episodes and shot lists: `episodes.md` in this folder.

---

## 1. Title, logline, tagline

**Title:** ADA MUST NOT PASS
(Alternatives if the owner prefers: "Doctor Freeze", "21 Days".)

**Logline:** When her rich aunt secretly needs her to fail JAMB so she can keep her late father's money, an 18-year-old who froze in last year's exam has 21 days, a cracked phone and one last chance to score 300 before she turns 19 and loses her future for good.

**Tagline (title card, captions):** Her aunty needs her to fail.

---

## 2. Why it is addictive

- **A villain people love to hate.** Aunty Nkem is the rich, smiling, church-going aunty every Nigerian has seen or heard about. Every episode she makes a bigger move: humiliation, burned notes, the market, a cheating rumour, lies to Mama, a padlock.
- **Humiliation, then payback.** The engine of every hit vertical drama. Ada is shamed in public (the 168, "Ada Expo") and wins it back in public (the 291 in front of Mr. Eze, the recording in front of the family).
- **A mystery from episode 1.** "As long as that girl fails, that money stays with us." What money? The answer (episode 6) changes everything.
- **A ticking clock on screen.** 21 days to JAMB, and her 19th birthday after that. The countdown returns in almost every episode.
- **The hero's own enemy is inside her.** She froze last year. Every exam scene asks: will she freeze again? (Episodes 4, 5 and 9 play on it.)
- **Two twists, both fair.** Episode 6: the money is Ada's own, and Aunty only gets it if Ada fails. Episode 7: the spoilt cousin already knew, and she is a prisoner too.
- **Comment bait in every episode:** "Should Bisola tell her mother?", "Who planted the expo?", "Did she freeze?"
- **Aura is never the subject.** It is the thing in Ada's hand when everything is taken from her. Fire can burn a notebook; it can't burn what is inside a phone.

## 3. Director's statement

**What it is really about:** who your future belongs to. Aunty believes the family's future (and money) is hers to manage because nobody ever helped her. Ada learns her future is hers, and that she doesn't have to win it alone.

**Tone in three words:** tense, warm, triumphant.

**Visual rules**
- Aunty's house is cold: white marble, glass, teal-white daylight, with wine red (Aunty's colour) in every frame she owns. The camera is still and symmetrical there; power keeps still.
- Ada's world is warm and cramped: the boys' quarters (BQ) under one amber bulb. Ada's colour is navy (her late father's polo).
- Handheld camera only when the world breaks (the fire, the locked door, the okada ride).
- Faces rule. Most shots are MCU and CU on 85 mm; wides only to establish or for the big turns. Two people are staged in depth (one near, one far), never side by side at the frame edges.
- Every freeze looks the same: ECU of Ada's fingers spread flat on a desk, breath stops, sound drains to a thin ring. The audience learns the image and dreads it.
- Look: ARRI Alexa 35 feel, Kodak Vision3 500T for night interiors, 250D for day. Rich dark skin with warm highlights and visible shadow detail, always.
- Each episode's first and last frames are its strongest images.

**Sound rules**
- Sound from frame one, always. No silent openings, no logo intros.
- **Aunty's keys** are her motif: a gold key ring that jingles whenever she is near. When the keys go silent (episode 9), her power is gone.
- **The freeze:** heartbeat plus a thin high ring; all room sound drops out.
- **The calm voice:** Aura's real lesson voice is the one gentle sound in Ada's hard world (episodes 1 and 3).
- Score: a low talking-drum pulse under soft strings. Every episode ends on a hard stop plus one deep bass hit, then black.
- Pidgin and Igbo lines always subtitled in English.

**Reference films (by their traits, never copied)**
- *Bad Genius* (2017): the exam as a thriller; hands, timers and sweat in close-up.
- *King of Boys* (2018): a smiling Lagos matriarch whose softness is the threat.
- The top ReelShort and DramaBox serials: humiliation in the first seconds, payback within two episodes, a hard cliffhanger every time.

| Element | In this series |

## 5. Characters bible

Locks for AI generation: every character keeps the SAME face, hair and outfit in every episode unless an exception is listed. Use the "Prompt anchor" text word for word in every generation, with the approved reference image of that character.

### ADA (Adaeze Okafor), the hero, 18
- **Face and body:** Igbo Nigerian girl, deep brown skin, oval face, high cheekbones, full lips, a small gap between her two front teeth (only seen when she truly smiles, saved for episode 10), dark brown eyes, no make-up. Slim, 1.63 m.
- **Hair:** natural black 4C hair in two thick cornrow braids running straight back to the nape. Never changes.
- **Wardrobe (all episodes):** her late father's navy-blue polo shirt, too big for her, sleeves past the elbows, small white embroidered letters "E.O." on the left chest; black knee-length A-line skirt; black rubber slippers at home, plain black flat sandals outside.
- **Exception:** episode 3 (market): adds a plain black headscarf tied at the nape over the braids, hiding a white wired earpiece.
- **Props:** old black Android phone with a crack running across the top-left corner; one white wired earpiece.
- **Voice:** low, quiet, few words, measured English; Pidgin only with Tolu. Never raises her voice until episode 9 ("Aunty! Open this door!").
- **Psychological gesture:** her right thumb rubs the "E.O." on the polo when she is scared or deciding. It is her father's hand.
- **The freeze (physical):** fingers spread flat on the desk, breath stops, eyes fixed and glassy, no blinking.
- **Movement quality:** light, sustained, bound (she takes up as little space as possible). By episode 9: direct and sudden.
- **Status:** low in Aunty's house; rises a notch in every episode she wins.
- **First action on screen:** eyes down, thumb on "E.O.", taking the humiliation without a word, then lifting her eyes: "Twenty-one days."
- **Arc:** silent and alone → accepts Tolu's help → trusts Mama → allies with Bisola → beats the freeze → claims her future.
- **Prompt anchor:** "Ada, 18-year-old Nigerian girl, deep brown skin, oval face, high cheekbones, small gap in front teeth, natural black hair in two thick cornrow braids straight back, oversized navy-blue polo shirt with small white embroidered letters E.O. on the left chest, black knee-length skirt."

### AUNTY NKEM (Mrs. Nkemdirim Okafor-Adeyemi), the villain, 49
- **Role:** Ada's father's younger sister, married to a Yoruba businessman who lives in Dubai (never seen). Manager of Emeka's estate. Owns a provisions stall at Oshodi and a duplex in Magodo GRA.
- **Face and body:** Igbo Nigerian woman, medium-dark brown skin, sharp face with strong cheekbones, thin arched drawn eyebrows, deep wine-red lipstick, small mole above the left lip. Tall (1.72 m), full-figured, upright posture.
- **Hair:** glossy black chin-length bob wig with a deep left side part. Never changes.
- **Wardrobe (all episodes):** wine-red silk kaftan (boubou) with gold embroidery around the neckline; large gold hoop earrings; a thin gold chain with a small cross; gold watch on the left wrist; wine-red nails; gold-trimmed slippers at home, gold flat sandals outside.
- **Props:** a big gold key ring (8 to 10 keys) she carries in her right hand or on her right wrist; a gold-cased phone; a gold-rimmed teacup at home.
- **Voice:** sweet, slow and smiling while she is cruel; Igbo-accented English; switches to Pidgin when she commands. She never shouts. Signature attitude: "I'm only helping."
- **Movement quality:** light, sustained, direct. Keeps very still; the keys do the moving.
- **Status:** highest in every room until episode 9, when her key ring hits the marble.
- **First action on screen:** slapping Ada's old 168 result slip on the dinner table, smiling at everyone but Ada.
- **Her humanity:** she grew up poorer than her brother and resented it. She says it once, in episode 10: "Emeka got everything. Nobody ever paid for my school."
- **Prompt anchor:** "Aunty Nkem, 49-year-old Nigerian woman, medium-dark brown skin, sharp face, thin arched eyebrows, deep wine-red lipstick, small mole above left lip, glossy black chin-length bob wig with deep left side part, wine-red silk kaftan with gold neckline embroidery, large gold hoop earrings, gold key ring in right hand."

### MAMA (Ngozi Okafor), the parent, 45
- **Role:** Ada's widowed mother, nurse at LASUTH, Ikeja, working night shifts to fund her UK nursing application. Needs IELTS band 7. Has always deferred to her late husband's family.
- **Face and body:** Igbo Nigerian woman, dark brown skin, round soft face, tired kind eyes, faint lines at the mouth, reading glasses on a red beaded chain. Medium build, 1.60 m.
- **Hair:** short low-cut natural hair, black with grey at the temples.
- **Wardrobe:** at work (episodes 5, 8): white nurse's dress, navy cardigan, nurse's fob watch pinned to the left chest, black low shoes. At home or out (episodes 9, 10): blue-and-gold ankara wrapper and blouse with a matching headtie.
- **Props:** phone in a red case; a pen behind her right ear at work.
- **Voice:** warm, gentle, Igbo-accented English; calls Ada "Nne m" (my dear, literally "my mother").
- **Gesture:** pushes her glasses up with her knuckle before she says something that matters.
- **Arc:** trusts Nkem → sees the report and asks "Which lessons?" → pays for Ada herself → goes over Nkem's head to Nne → band 7.5.
- **Prompt anchor:** "Mama Ngozi, 45-year-old Nigerian nurse, dark brown skin, round kind face, reading glasses on a red beaded chain, short natural hair with grey temples, white nurse's dress with navy cardigan."

### BISOLA (Bisola Adeyemi), the cousin, 17
- **Role:** Aunty Nkem's only child, writing JAMB the same Saturday. Looks spoilt; is secretly crushed. Wants Fine Art; her mother insists on Medicine.
- **Face and body:** Nigerian girl, medium brown skin (a shade lighter than Ada), round face, full cheeks, glossy lip gloss, gold stud earrings. 1.65 m.
- **Hair:** waist-length honey-brown knotless braids, usually over one shoulder.
- **Wardrobe (all episodes):** oversized pastel-pink hoodie, white jeans, white sneakers; white wireless earbuds.
- **Props:** an iPad with a few art stickers on the case; she sketches on it when nervous (plant for Fine Art). Her phone (used to record in episode 7).
- **Voice:** Lagos-posh English, sarcastic, short. When the act drops (episode 7 on), softer and faster, close to tears.
- **Arc:** mocks Ada ("Doctor Freeze") → catches her (ep 6) → twist: already knew (ep 7) → exposes her own mother (ep 9) → scores 236, her own score (ep 10).
- **Prompt anchor:** "Bisola, 17-year-old Nigerian girl, medium brown skin, round face, lip gloss, waist-length honey-brown knotless braids over one shoulder, oversized pastel-pink hoodie, white jeans, white sneakers."

### TOLU (Tolulope Bakare), the best friend, 18
- **Role:** Ada's best friend from their old street in Surulere. Broke, funny, loyal. Runs the "Surulere Squad" group mock. Weak at WAEC oral English (says "sheep" for "ship").
- **Face and body:** Yoruba Nigerian boy, dark skin, long face, wide grin with a slight overbite. Tall and skinny, 1.85 m.
- **Hair:** low fade with a razor part line on the left.
- **Wardrobe (all episodes):** faded red football replica jersey (no club badge or sponsor logo visible), black joggers, black slides.
- **Props:** a fat black power bank always in his hand or pocket; his uncle's red okada (episode 9).
- **Voice:** fast, loud Pidgin, jokes under pressure.
- **Arc:** comic relief → the one who saves her twice (the recordings, the leaderboard) → says "ship" right in episode 10.
- **Prompt anchor:** "Tolu, 18-year-old Nigerian boy, dark skin, long face, wide grin, tall and skinny, low fade haircut with razor part on the left, faded red football jersey with no logos, black joggers, black slides."

### MR. EZE, the teacher, 63
- **Role:** retired maths teacher who runs the JAMB class at a church hall in Surulere. Strict and fair; first accuses Ada, then clears her.
- **Look:** dark skin, short grey beard, wire-rimmed glasses, bald crown with grey sides; brown short-sleeved safari suit; chalk dust on his fingers.
- **Voice:** deep, slow, teacher's English; calls her "my daughter".
- **Prompt anchor:** "Mr. Eze, 63-year-old Nigerian teacher, dark skin, short grey beard, wire-rimmed glasses, bald crown, brown short-sleeved safari suit."

### NNE (Mama Okafor), the grandmother, 78
- **Role:** mother of Emeka and Nkem; lives in Nnewi; the only person Nkem fears. Reads Igbo (from her Igbo Bible), not English.
- **Look:** small and thin, dark skin, deeply lined face, white headtie, white lace blouse, dark-blue george wrapper, carved wooden walking stick, thick reading glasses.

## 9. Brand guardrails and production rules

- **This series departs from the ad formula on purpose.** The owner asked for entertainment that doesn't look like selling, so episodes 1 to 9 open on drama, not on the "Do you want to...?" question, and carry no price or next step. Everything else in `../aura-video-formula.md` still applies.
- **The word "Aura" is never spoken.** The app appears only as real screens in a character's hand. The name and price appear only in the episode 10 end card, the posting captions' last line, and a pinned comment.
- **No promises.** Nobody says the app will get you a score. Ada's 301 is her story; the finale card reads "Dramatisation."
- **App screens are real recordings** of the live app (see the capture list below), never AI-made, 1 to 3 seconds per episode, held long enough to read one thing.
- **AI label.** If any character is AI-generated, every post is labelled as AI-generated. The formula prefers real actors; casting is an owner decision (section 11).
- **Frame:** 1080 x 1920, 24 fps. Faces and text between y = 220 and y = 1440 px, away from the right edge. Subtitles burned in, bottom line no lower than y = 1400.
- **Title card:** "ADA MUST NOT PASS · EP n" for 1.5 s at the top of the frame, over picture, starting at about 2 s. Last 1 s of episodes 1 to 9: "Episode n+1 tomorrow".
- **Real exam bodies:** no JAMB or WAEC logos or official screens are recreated. The expo sheet and result SMS are plain text.
- **Posting:** one episode a day at 6:30pm WAT, TikTok, Reels and Shorts, as a playlist "Ada Must Not Pass". Owner approves every post first.
- **Before anything is generated:** a full cost quote at exact settings, one test shot first, then a yes on that number.
