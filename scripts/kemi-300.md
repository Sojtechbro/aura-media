## 25-second cut

0–3s hook ("Do you want to score 300 and above in JAMB? Let me tell you about Kemi.") → 3–7s the 186 and Mum's "no money for lesson" → 7–17s Aura montage (wrong answer, Listen to the lesson, Watch it as a video, timed mock, Mum's report) → 17–21s result: 301, Mum's hug → 21–25s CTA.

## Rules this script follows
- Hook = the outcome, as a plain yes/no question, spoken and on screen from frame one.
- The narrator never promises a score. Aura "helps you prepare"; Kemi's result is her story, labelled as a dramatisation.
- Features appear only as how Kemi got there.
- Full sentences, no riddles. Pidgin only in Mum's lines, always subtitled.
- No "free". The price is the real Exam pass price.
- If the characters are AI-made, label the post as AI.
