# "Two Heads": David's 120 to 350, version 3 (production script)

- **Story and lines:** the owner's, 19 Nov 2026. Version 3 adds his notes:
  - a middle-class family and homes;
  - real emotion with no overacting, except Mama David in scene 1, who is visibly upset, angry and shouting (owner, 19 Nov);
  - Susan is a girl;
  - a desktop montage of her using Aura, using the real app recordings;
  - the line about the JAMB exam simulation.
- **Look:** live action with photoreal Nigerian actors, filmed like a quality Nollywood drama, natural light, 9:16. No cartoon or animation.
- **Length:** about 2 minutes 15 seconds.
- **On screen:** subtitles on every line, and "Dramatisation" on the 350. No price, pass or credits wording anywhere.

## Cast (lock one sheet each; nobody's face, hair, body or clothes may change between shots)
- **Mama David (Mrs Adeyemi)**, about 45. A bank manager.
  - Medium-brown skin and a short, neat relaxed bob.
  - Scene 1: a navy work dress with a slim gold watch on her LEFT wrist, small gold stud earrings, and her work ID lanyard just taken off, in her hand.
  - Scenes 2 to 4: a smart wine-red ankara two-piece, the same watch and the same earrings.
  - She is composed by nature, which makes it hit harder when she breaks: in scene 1 she is visibly upset, angry and shouting, with real tears. It is a real mother losing her temper at home, not a cartoon.
- **David Adeyemi**, 18.
  - Deep-brown skin, a neat low cut, and slim.
  - Scenes 1 and 3: a plain grey T-shirt and black joggers.
  - Scene 4: a white T-shirt.
  - He is quiet, ashamed and avoids her eyes.
- **Mama Susan (Mrs Bello)**, about 47. A doctor.
  - Dark-brown skin, shoulder-length braids in a low bun, small gold hoops, and a soft green kaftan.
  - Warm and calm.
- **Susan Bello**, about 18, a girl.
  - Deep-brown skin, knotless braids to her shoulders tied half up, and tiny silver studs.
  - At home: a lilac T-shirt and jeans.
  - In the flashback: a cream hoodie, with her braids down.
  - Bright, confident and respectful. She kneels slightly to greet an elder, the Yoruba way.

## Sets (middle class, all clean and lived-in, no luxury or poverty clichés)
- **S1, Adeyemi living room, Magodo GRA, Lagos.**
  - Grey L-shaped sofa, patterned rug, a wall-mounted TV (off) and a split AC unit.
  - Framed family portrait, a potted snake plant, cream curtains and a polished tiled floor.
  - The dining table can be seen behind.
  - Warm table lamps in the evening; soft daylight in the morning.
- **S2, Bello living room, a Lekki terrace house.**
  - Bright and modern: a cream sofa, cushions, a bookshelf and a coffee table with a tray of drinks. Afternoon daylight through big windows.
- **S3, Susan's bedroom study desk** (flashback).
  - A white desk, an unbranded silver laptop, a desk lamp, a ring-bound notebook and a water bottle.
  - Evening, lit by the lamp and the screen.

## The real app recordings (never AI-made or redrawn)
These are recorded from the live Aura web app at desktop size. In S3 they are composited onto Susan's laptop screen with a clean corner-pin, with screen glow on her face. For insert close-ups, the recording fills the frame, with a slow push-in onto the part being talked about.
- **R1 `desk_cbt.mp4`, the JAMB CBT simulation.**
  - 0–5.6s: the "Practise JAMB the way you'll sit it" setup with every subject.
  - 5.6s: "Start the exam" opens the CBT screen, with the timer, subject tabs, question grid and Next/Submit.
  - 8.5s: she answers.
  - 13.2s: she jumps to question 7.
  - 15.2s: she switches to the Mathematics tab.
- **R2 `desk_practice_lesson_video.mp4`, missed question to lesson to video.**
  - 0s: a past question.
  - 2.0s: the wrong answer turns red.
  - 4.7s: "Take a quick lesson" opens the detailed lesson.
  - 7.3s: Listen, with the words lighting up.
  - 14.4s: "Watch it as a video".
  - 16.2s: the video lesson plays.
- **R3 `desk_plan_topics.mp4`, subjects broken into topics.**
  - 3.3s: JAMB and four subjects.
  - 8.6s: "Make my plan".
  - 12.9s onwards: the day-by-day list of small topics.
- **Audio sync rule:** each sentence of Susan's voice-over starts on the exact moment its action happens in the recording, at the times above.

## SCENE 1: THE SCOLDING (S1, evening)

**Beats:**
1. To confront: shock, then the first shout.
2. To shame: the money and the second failure, shouting, tears coming.
3. To wound: "your mates", the loudest point, then her voice cracks into tears.

**Status:** Mama David stands, so she is high. David sits, so he is low, and stays low.

| Shot | Picture and acting | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Medium. Mama David has just come in from work and is still holding her lanyard in her RIGHT hand. Her eyes are on her phone in her LEFT hand. She stops dead. Her mouth falls open; her breathing gets fast. David sits on the sofa behind her in soft focus. | MAMA DAVID *(shocked, voice rising)*: One hundred and twenty? |
| 1B | Close on Mama David. Her eyes snap from the phone to him. The lanyard drops from her RIGHT hand. She SHOUTS, leaning toward him from the waist. | *(shouting)* David! One hundred and twenty?! |
| 1C | Close on David. He flinches at the shout. His eyes drop to the floor. His RIGHT thumb picks at the sofa seam. He swallows. | *(silence)* |
| 1D | Medium two-shot. She strides to him and shakes the phone at him at chest height, her LEFT hand jabbing toward the screen. Her voice is loud and sharp. | *(shouting)* And you want to study Medicine? Medicine, with one hundred and twenty?! |
| 1E | Close on Mama David. She is shouting at full voice, eyes wet and red, chest heaving. She slaps her palms together hard twice on "second time". Her voice cracks on the last "The second time!" and a tear runs down her RIGHT cheek. | *(shouting, close to tears)* After all the money I have spent on lesson teachers, and everything, this is the second time you are failing JAMB! The second time! |
| 1F | Close on Mama David. She puts her RIGHT hand on her head in distress and turns half away, then swings back to him, still loud, her face twisted with hurt. A sharp hiss. | *(shouting, hurt)* What kind of thing is this? You don't have any shame? |
| 1G | Over David's shoulder onto her. She bends down close to his face and jabs two fingers of her RIGHT hand at her own temple. This is the loudest she gets; tears are running now. | *(shouting at the top of her voice)* Your mates are scoring three hundred. Do they have two heads? Ehn? |
| 1H | Close on David. He still won't look up. His jaw tightens; his eyes fill. | MAMA DAVID *(off, voice breaking into a sob)*: Do they have two heads? |

**Acting notes:**
- Owner direction (19 Nov): Mama David is VISIBLY upset, angry and shouting, and emotional. Real loud shouting, wet red eyes, real tears from 1E onward, heavy breathing, hands moving (lanyard drop, palm slaps, hand on head, finger to temple).
- Her anger comes from love and fear for his future, so under the shouting she is hurt, and by 1H the shout breaks into a sob.
- Keep it a real mother, not a cartoon: no eye-rolling, no exaggerated faces, no comedy. Mouth fully visible on her lines for lip-sync. Her voice is the same voice as in scenes 2 to 4, just loud.
- Scenes 2 to 4 stay natural and understated.

## Rules for production
- **Real people, real physics.**
  - Phones and cups have weight, hands hold them correctly, and the right number of fingers.
  - No morphing faces, hair, clothes or bodies.
  - Nobody looks into the lens.
  - No extra people.
  - No brand logos on phones or laptops.
- **One fixed voice per character in every shot**, Nigerian English accent matched to age. Lip-sync must be clean.
- **App screens only from the real recordings.** Never generate, redraw or morph an app screen.
- Subtitles burned in, clear of the bottom 25% and the right edge.
